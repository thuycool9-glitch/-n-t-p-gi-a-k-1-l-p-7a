HOW TO PLAY

1. Type your name, choose the number of questions and a mode.
2. Answer each question, then press SUBMIT ANSWER.
3. Correct answer = +10. 3 correct answers in a row = +5 streak bonus.
4. Practice Mode: no timer, no penalty. Challenge Mode: timer on, and 50/50 hint on multiple-choice costs 5 points.
5. Finish all 6 rounds to earn badges and stars ⭐. Use REVIEW WRONG ANSWERS at the end.
